# Architecture Register

## Purpose

This is the canonical control register for the BIAN Adoption & Engineering
Platform. It records the decisions, active questions, risks, assumptions,
dependencies, evidence gaps, architecture requirements, work items, and issues
that govern product and architecture progression.

Architecture and product documents may explain an item in context and reference
its identifier. They must not maintain a competing status, owner, resolution, or
decision. If a conflict exists, this register governs until the conflict is
resolved explicitly.

## Register rules

- Every governed record has a stable identifier that is never reused.
- The register retains resolved, closed, rejected, and superseded records.
- The accountable owner is a role until a named person or group is formally
  assigned. It must use the canonical role vocabulary below.
- Gate fields and next-review triggers must use a canonical `GAT` identifier.
- Origin, derivation and review history must remain attributable through the
  provenance ledger or a more specific record-level entry.
- A record changes state only when evidence or an authorised decision supports
  the change.
- A document reference does not close or approve a record.
- Related records must reference each other where the relationship affects a
  decision or gate.
- Discovery prompts and scenario-review questions remain reusable research
  material. Promote one into this register only when it becomes an active
  project question, risk, requirement, evidence gap, or work item.
- Review affected records with every material architecture change and before
  each canonical gate.
- Requirement acceptance confirms that wording, scope, owner, gate and expected
  evidence are agreed. It does not claim that the requirement has been
  satisfied or that its expected evidence has been produced.
- A gate's blocking records require an explicit disposition and a visible
  consequence at that gate. A record whose closure depends on a later gate is
  carried as a limitation rather than making the earlier gate impossible to
  pass, unless the earlier gate's exit criteria expressly require closure.
- Risk likelihood and impact use the coarse `Low`, `Medium` and `High` scale
  until a later risk method is approved. They are current qualitative
  architecture assessments, not measured probability or independent evidence.
  `Mitigating` means a response exists or is in progress, not that the exposure
  has been removed. `Accepted` requires a recorded project-owner decision. If a
  risk occurs, create and link an `ISS` record rather than silently closing or
  rewriting the risk.

role.

owner performs each staffed role. This is role separation for accountability,
not evidence of independent challenge or segregation of duties.

## Requirements

Requirements are maintained only in this register. The
[requirements and traceability model](../architecture/REQUIREMENTS_AND_TRACEABILITY.md)
explains their hierarchy, derivation, lifecycle, and use without duplicating
their status or authoritative wording.

An `Accepted` requirement is part of the governed baseline because its wording,
scope, owner, delivery gate and expected evidence are agreed. Acceptance does
not mean that the requirement has been implemented or that the evidence named
in the `Acceptance evidence` column has been produced. Proposed and Deferred
requirements retain their stated obligations without entering the accepted
baseline at the current review point.

decision. Acceptance does not mean that the future product already satisfies
