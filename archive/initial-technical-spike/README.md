# Initial technical spike — archived

## Status

**Superseded and retained for historical reference only.**

This technical experiment was created before the product use cases and
conceptual architecture were properly defined. None of its technology, model,
architecture, ADR, implementation, or roadmap decisions are approved for the
future product.

The active product definition is located at the repository root under
`product/`.

## Potentially reusable principles

The following ideas may be reconsidered during the future architecture phase:

- canonical model first;
- source provenance and explicit versioning;
- generated-versus-owned asset separation;
- profile-driven security;
- evidence-based assurance;
- deterministic generation; and
- explicit source-data and licensing considerations.

Their presence here does not mean their earlier design is accepted.

## Premature choices requiring fresh evaluation

- Python and the command-line implementation;
- file-based storage;
- the proposed canonical schema and relationship vocabulary;
- repository and package structure;
- synthetic fixtures and generated examples;
- the original ADRs and technical roadmap; and
- the definition of the initial vertical slice.

Do not extend this prototype while product discovery and conceptual architecture
remain incomplete.
